Vice Heat is a top-down, GTA 1/2-style open-world crime game set in Neon Bay, an original neon coastal city of downtown towers, a palm-lined beachfront strip and an industrial corner, rendered on HTML canvas in a dusk magenta-and-cyan palette with vanilla JavaScript and zero network requests. Explore on foot or steal and drive any car — with drifting, skid marks, damage and smoke — while traffic flows, pedestrians wander and flee, and a 1–5 star wanted system sends police ramming after you until a BUSTED meter fills or you break line of sight and cool off. Punch passers-by, find the pistol pickup and shoot with mouse aim, and work a four-mission story chain for fixer Mama Rosa — a car theft delivery, a timed package run, a 3-star escape to the safehouse and a final high-pressure getaway — paid in cash, guided by phone messages, map markers and a live minimap, with free roam and repeatable taxi fares afterwards, localStorage saves, and a fully WebAudio-synthesised soundtrack of engine hum, sirens, skids and jingles.
## Run it
```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080
```
